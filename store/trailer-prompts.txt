=========================================================================
READ THIS FIRST -- THREE THINGS THE SOURCE ART DICTATES
=========================================================================

1. THE STILLS ARE NOT ALL VERTICAL, AND YOU SHOULD NOT MAKE THEM VERTICAL.
   Already 9:16, use as they are:   marsh.jpg (720x1556)
                                    churchyard.jpg (720x1556)
                                    study.jpg (432x934)
   Square 1:1:                      obj-receiver.jpg, obj-stairs.jpg,
                                    obj-mast.jpg, obj-logbook.jpg
   Generate the square ones AT 1:1 and place them centred on a black
   1080x1920 canvas in the edit. Do NOT ask any model to outpaint them to
   vertical. Outpainting invents wall, floor and ceiling that were never in
   the book, and the invented part never matches the halftone. Black above and
   below is not a compromise here: the book's own doctrine is that whitespace
   is fear and single elements float in acres of empty page. Letterboxing IS
   the house style.

2. THE RECEIVER'S DIAL ALREADY CARRIES PRINTED NUMBERS.
   They sit small and soft in the source and read as texture, which is fine.
   What must never happen: the needle settling anywhere a viewer could read,
   or the model sharpening those markings into legible figures. The book's
   frequencies are puzzle answers. Keep the dial glass soft, keep the needle
   drifting and unresolved, and reject any take where a number becomes
   readable. Note that MARKETING.md's teaser sketch says the needle drifts
   "to 4625" -- ignore that. 4625 is the answer to a Broadcast One gate and
   must never appear in public video.

3. THE MARSH LIGHT IS ALREADY LIT.
   TRAILER.md asks for "one distant amber light comes on halfway through".
   In marsh.jpg the light at the mast top is already burning. To get the
   switch-on you need start-and-end keyframes: duplicate the still, paint the
   light out of the copy in any editor, then feed dark-frame as start and
   original as end and let the model interpolate. That is the Luma trick in
   TRAILER.md's tooling section. If that is too much work, use the alternative
   motion given in SHOT 6 instead, which needs no keyframes.

Universal settings for every shot: mute all generated audio, no upscaling,
no sharpening, 3-4 takes each and KEEP THE LEAST ANIMATED ONE.

SUBJECT: a war-surplus shortwave radio receiver from the early 1960s, seen

else in frame moves. No camera shake, no handheld drift, no rack focus, no

the only colour in frame and must stay dim.

=========================================================================
THE TITLE CARD IS NOT GENERATED
=========================================================================
Never let a model make your typography. It hallucinates letterforms and the
result is the one frame that tells a viewer this was machine-made.

Render the end card from the app itself: open Number Nine, let the title screen
sit, and screen-record it on device. That gives you the real typeface, the real
amber, and the real glowing 9, and it costs nothing. Drop that recording in as
the final two to three seconds.

If you want a plain card instead, set it in the book's own serif on near-black
with the amber 9, in Resolve. Still not generated.

=========================================================================
BEFORE YOU PUBLISH ANYTHING
=========================================================================
- Watch it once looking ONLY at the dial and the headstones. If a number or a
  name resolved, recut. That is the whole spoiler risk in one sentence.
- No competitor's mark in the title, description or tags of the upload. The
  trademark rule covers video metadata exactly as it covers store metadata.
- No accessibility claim in the description.
- The App Store preview video has its own size and duration rules and they are
  NOT the screenshot rules. Read them off the ASC upload box. Assuming the
  general case instead of reading the page already cost a redo on screenshots.
- Apple additionally requires App Store preview footage to be captured from the
  app itself. A mood trailer made from these shots is fine for social and NOT
  acceptable as your App Store preview. Those are two different deliverables.
