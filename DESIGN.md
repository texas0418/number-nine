# Number Nine — design doctrine

Distilled from studying DEVICE 6 (Simogo, 2013) frame by frame — Simon's bar:
"elegant, and the movement with the sound works really well together."

## Text is architecture (Simon's rule: never rotate "just because")

Every typographic deviation must be DIEGETIC — motivated by the story's
physical space or by what the text IS in-fiction:

- Text runs downward when a character descends. It runs back up when they
  return. Re-reading = physically walking back through the space.
- Columns fork because the space forks (two doors, two rooms).
- Mirrored/upside-down text belongs to the station: she speaks turned
  around. That is a rule of the world (and a puzzle), not a style.
- Rooms get labels: small-caps, letter-spaced, underlined headers, like
  places on a survey map (DEVICE 6: "NORTHWEST GUEST ROOM").

## Book, not app

- Prose is justified, book-set serif at a comfortable measure. Dialogue
  lives inside paragraphs. No UI furniture near the prose.
- Inner thoughts are centered italics floating in whitespace, set apart
  from narration ("Where in the world am I?").
- Whitespace is fear. Single small elements are allowed to float in acres
  of empty page. Never fill space.
- Chapters open on a frontispiece plate: chapter number in small caps, a
  circular motif, an italic title (DEVICE 6's orange chapter cards).
- Interactive things are typographic: an italic noun, a thin guide arrow,
  a widget drawn in the page's own ink. Wrong answers get atmosphere,
  never error copy.
- Media sits IN the text column and obeys its geometry — images abut and
  wrap, art rotates with the corridor.

## Sound moves with the page

- The bed (static, hum, room tone) follows scroll position — approaching
  something changes what you hear before you read it.
- Widgets are instruments: tuning sweeps the static; a dialed phone rings
  through the bed; the lock "thunk" is haptic + ident, never a toast.
- Silence is a cue in its own right (the scariest one).
- Headphones-first: stereo placement is content, not polish.

## Palette

DEVICE 6 is warm paper + black book type + halftone photo collage + one
red accent. Number Nine keeps its own identity — a night radio: near-black
room, bone serif, phosphor glow, one amber accent (the dial). The print
sensibility transfers; the palette does not need to.
